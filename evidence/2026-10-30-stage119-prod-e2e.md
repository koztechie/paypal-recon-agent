## Discoveries & incidents (honest log)
1. **`recordExternalPayment` DOES fire the real `INVOICING.INVOICE.PAID` webhook.**
   This resolves stage97's open question: yesterday's missing payments row was purely
   the dead ngrok tunnel, not the missing event. A $1 probe invoice
   (`INV2-4LUW-TM5G-VMHC-PSB7`) proved it — and, with local ngrok dead, the event
   still reached PROD (webhook independence confirmed).
2. **Prod DB is ephemeral — every deploy wipes it.** First poll after the stage-119
   deploy returned `0` rows (the stage-118 CREATED event was gone); the fresh E2E
   events then rebuilt the ledger. Operational rule: finish all data-producing pushes
   BEFORE recording prod demos, or accept a clean-slate ledger (also nice for demos).
3. **ngrok died again mid-session** (process gone) → local webhook events were lost
   for ~20 min → restarted (`ngrok start ecwid-dev`, /health 200). The prod webhook
   was unaffected the whole time — the point of stage 118.
4. Observation for later (NOT blocking): prod `INVOICE` rows upserted by the
   reconciliation path show `amount 0.00` (both rows), while PAYMENT rows carry the
   real amounts. Worth a look before prod-facing invoice demos (follow-up candidate).
5. `/api/recon/run` is POST-only and incremental (unreconciled payments only) —
   consistent with the existing unauthenticated-demo API posture (nl/explain/action).
